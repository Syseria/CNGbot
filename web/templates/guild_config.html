5. Corporation Wallet Donations Import

Paste your corporation wallet journal filtered on Player Donation (from the in-game Corporation Wallet → Journal tab) or upload a .txt file to backfill historical donations. You can paste multiple times (e.g. for individual past months or the last 30 days); duplicate donations are automatically detected and skipped.
